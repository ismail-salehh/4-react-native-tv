# Navigation on TV

### What works: sidebar and top tabs

- **Sidebar (left menu).** Collapsed to icons while the user is in content; expands with labels when focus enters it. Reach it by pressing Left from the first column, or with Back. Good for many sections (Home, Movies, Series, Search, Settings).
- **Top tabs.** A row of sections at the top of the screen. Reach them by pressing Up from the first rail, or with Back. Good for a few sections (3–6).

Both keep the menu **one or two presses away** and in a fixed place the user learns.

When focus enters the menu, put it on the **current** section (selected), not the first item. When it leaves, return focus to where the user was in the content.

### What does not work

- **Swipe gestures.** There is no touch screen. A swipe on the Siri Remote touch surface is just a direction to the focus engine, not a gesture you can rely on, and other remotes have no touch surface at all.
- **Hamburger menus.** Hidden behind an icon, costing extra presses to discover.
- **Bottom tab bars.** Far from where focus usually is, and often in the overscan area.
- **Pull to refresh, long scroll pages, drag and drop.** All need a touch or pointer.
