# Safe areas, overscan, TV-safe margins

Some TVs **crop the edges** of the picture (overscan), and many users zoom the picture. Anything near the edge may be cut off.

| Guideline | Horizontal | Vertical |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android TV | 48 dp each side (of 960 dp) | 27 dp top and bottom (of 540 dp) |
| Apple TV | 80 pt each side | 60 pt top and bottom |
| Same thing at 1920×1080 design size | ~96 px (5%) | ~54 px (5%) |

Rules:

- **Text, buttons, and anything focusable go inside the safe area.**
- **Backgrounds and artwork go full bleed.** A hero image that stops at the safe margin looks like a framed picture.
- **Rails may scroll past the edge.** The first card starts at the safe margin, and later cards can run off the right edge to show there is more.
- Put the margins in one place (`theme.safe.x`, `theme.safe.y`) and use them on every screen. Don't hand-type 96 in each file.

```tsx
export const safe = { x: s(96), y: s(54) };

<View style={{ flex: 1, paddingHorizontal: safe.x, paddingVertical: safe.y }}>
```
